02 Bayesian modelling · Data science

Pollution Blame Ledger

A hierarchical Bayesian model that attributes Delhi's PM2.5 pollution to traffic, crop burning, dust, industry and background — with 90% credible intervals and per-source health burden.

  • NUTS sampling in NumPyro over hourly data from 6 CPCB stations, NASA FIRMS fires and meteorology
  • Tracer-based features to separate overlapping sources
  • Intervals held the true value 80% of the time on stress tests, vs 23% for an NNLS baseline
  • Python
  • NumPyro
  • Bayesian inference
  • Pandas
